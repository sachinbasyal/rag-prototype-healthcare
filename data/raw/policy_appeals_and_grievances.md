# Policy: Appeals and Grievances

## Purpose
Define the process for handling appeals and grievances to ensure timely and fair resolution.

## Scope
Applies to member-submitted and provider-submitted appeals for denied claims and coverage decisions.

## Definitions
- Appeal: Request to reconsider a claim or coverage decision.
- Grievance: Complaint about service quality, access, or member experience not directly tied to claim payment.

## Policy Rules
1. Appeals must be submitted in writing unless an urgent verbal request is allowed by policy.
2. The organization must acknowledge receipt of an appeal within 5 calendar days.
3. Standard appeals are resolved within 30 calendar days from receipt.
4. Expedited appeals are resolved within 72 hours when delay could seriously jeopardize health.

## Exceptions
- Incomplete submissions may be pended for additional documents for up to 10 calendar days.
- If required documentation is not received, the case may be administratively closed.

## Required Documentation
- Appeal form or written request
- Original denial notice
- Clinical notes or supporting records
- Provider letter of medical necessity (if applicable)

## Escalation Path
- Level 1: Appeals Specialist
- Level 2: Clinical Reviewer
- Level 3: Appeals Committee
