# Policy: Prior Authorization

## Purpose
Define when prior authorization is required and how prior authorization requests must be submitted, reviewed, and communicated before services are rendered.

## Scope
Applies to providers, members, and internal utilization-management staff for pre-service authorization requests.

## Definitions
- Prior Authorization (PA): Pre-service approval required for selected services, procedures, tests, medications, or devices.
- Standard PA Request: A non-urgent request reviewed under routine timelines.
- Expedited PA Request: A request reviewed under accelerated timelines when delay could seriously jeopardize health.
- PA Determination: Final status of approved, modified, pended, or not approved.

## Services Commonly Requiring Prior Authorization
1. Advanced imaging (e.g., MRI, CT, PET)
2. Elective inpatient admissions
3. Selected outpatient procedures
4. Specialty medications
5. Durable medical equipment above policy thresholds

## Submission Requirements
1. Request must include member identifiers, ordering provider details, diagnosis codes, and requested service codes.
2. Clinical documentation must support medical necessity (history, exam findings, prior treatment, rationale).
3. Incomplete submissions are pended and returned for additional information.
4. Requests must be submitted through approved channels (portal, fax, or designated intake process).

## Review Process
1. Intake validation confirms member eligibility and request completeness.
2. Clinical review is performed against medical-necessity criteria.
3. If criteria are met, the request is approved for the authorized scope and timeframe.
4. If criteria are not met, the request may be not approved with documented rationale.
5. Pended requests are re-reviewed after required documents are received.

## Timelines / SLA
- Standard PA decision: within 5 business days of complete submission
- Expedited PA decision: within 72 hours of complete submission
- Pend notification: within 2 business days of identifying missing information

## Communication Requirements
1. Determination notices must include service requested, decision status, effective dates, and rationale.
2. Notices must include next-step instructions for reconsideration or appeal pathways when applicable.
3. Provider and member communications must be logged for audit traceability.

## Required Documentation Checklist
- PA request form (complete)
- Clinical notes supporting necessity
- Relevant lab/imaging results (if applicable)
- Prior treatment history and outcomes
- Procedure/order details and expected setting of care

## Exclusions
- Emergency stabilization services do not require prior authorization at point of care.
- Services explicitly listed as no-authorization-required in current policy schedules are excluded.
