# SOP: Claim Intake Workflow

## Purpose
Operational workflow for receiving, validating, and routing incoming claims.

## Scope
Applies to members, providers, and internal operations teams for standard and urgent processing scenarios.

## Definitions
- Appeal: A request to reconsider a decision on a claim or coverage.
- Grievance: A complaint about service quality or access not directly tied to claim payment.
- Prior Authorization: Pre-approval required before selected services or medications.

## Policy / Procedure Rules
1. Requests must include complete member/provider identifiers and service details.
2. Incomplete submissions are pended for documentation; unresolved pends may be closed administratively.
3. Decisions must be documented with rationale and traceable references to policy sections.
4. Denial communications should include corrective next steps and escalation channels.

## Exceptions
- Urgent/expedited handling applies when delay may risk member health.
- Retrospective review may be considered for emergency stabilization events.
- Out-of-network exceptions may apply when no in-network equivalent is reasonably available.

## Timelines / SLA
- Acknowledgement of receipt: within 3 calendar days
- Standard review: within 18 business days
- Appeal resolution target: within 30 calendar days
- Expedited handling target: within 72 hours

## Required Documentation
- Request form (or equivalent structured request)
- Clinical notes and supporting records (if applicable)
- Relevant coding/billing details
- Prior decision notice (for appeals)

## Denial/Decision Codes
- D001: Missing prior authorization
- D002: Incomplete supporting documentation
- D003: Service not covered under current policy
- D004: Duplicate submission detected

## Escalation Path
- Level 1: Operations Specialist
- Level 2: Clinical/Technical Reviewer
- Level 3: Appeals or Governance Committee
